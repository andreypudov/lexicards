#+TITLE: LexiCards User Guide
#+OPTIONS: toc:2

* The menu bar

LexiCards has no Dock icon or main window. Everything starts from its icon in the menu bar:

| Item                   | What it does                                                                |
|------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| *Vocabulary*           | Opens Settings on the Vocabulary tab                                        |
| *Show Card*            | Shows or hides the card; the choice is remembered                           |
| *Recall Now*           | Starts a recall session; becomes *End Recall* while a session is running    |
| *Pronounce Cards*      | Turns speech on or off; off by default                                      |
| *Settings…*            | Opens Settings on the Application tab                                       |
| *Quit LexiCards*       | Quits the app                                                               |

* The card

The card floats above other windows and stays available across Spaces and full-screen apps. It starts near the lower-right corner of the screen, and its position, width, and visibility are remembered between launches.

- *Move it:* drag anywhere on the card.
- *Resize it:* drag the left or right side. Width ranges from 240 to 800 points, and the height follows the text.
- *Style it:* choose a font and size for each line in Settings.

** Fading under the pointer

By default, the card fades when the pointer rests on it, and clicks pass through to whatever is underneath. Hold *Option* to bring it back so you can move, resize, or click it. You can turn this off in Settings. A recall session doesn't start fading, but a card that was already faded when the session began stays faded until the pointer leaves it.

* Rotation

The card shows a new random entry every eight seconds by default, and never repeats the entry already on screen. You can set the interval from 2 to 300 seconds in Settings. Rotation pauses during a recall session.

* Recall

A recall session turns the card into a short test of up to ten entries, drawn at random without repeats. Each card asks for either the translation or the original, chosen at random.

1. The question appears, with a question mark in place of the answer.
2. Press *Reveal* to show the answer.
3. Press *Next* to move on. The counter at the top shows your progress.

The card returns to the rotation after the last word. The card changes size when a session starts and ends, growing away from the nearest screen edges and keeping that size during the session.

Answers aren't scored or recorded, and entries are drawn at random rather than by what you know.

** Starting a session

- *Automatically:* every 30 minutes by default. You can set the interval from 1 to 240 minutes, or turn off *Start recall automatically* in Settings. The interval counts from the end of the previous session. Automatic sessions don't appear while the card is hidden.
- *On demand:* choose *Recall Now* from the menu. If the card is hidden, it appears for that session only.

** Ending a session early

Press the *×* on the card, or choose *End Recall* from the menu. The next automatic session then comes a full interval later. Hiding the card or changing to another vocabulary also ends the session.

* Pronunciation

*Pronounce Cards* in the menu turns speech on and off. Settings then control what is spoken:

- *Speak cards in the rotation:* speaks each new card.
- *Speak cards during recall:* speaks each side as it appears, so an answer is never read out before you reveal it.
- *Speak:* chooses *Word only* or *Word and translation*.
- *Voice:* *Automatic* picks voices from the languages detected in the vocabulary. Choosing a specific voice uses it for both sides, and *Try* plays a sample.

The speaker button on a recall card works whenever you press it, even with speech turned off. It pronounces the original once that is on screen, and otherwise the translation, so it can't give away a hidden answer.

* Vocabularies

The Vocabulary tab in Settings lists the available vocabularies and shows the entries in the selected one.

- *Built-in:* the app ships with two Genki I lists and starts on the first one.
- *Added:* press *Add CSV…* to add your own file, and *Remove* to take it off the list. Removing doesn't delete the file.

LexiCards reads your file in place rather than copying it, and reloads it from there at launch. A file with no usable rows isn't added.

** CSV format

- UTF-8 text with one entry per line
- Original in the first column, translation in the second
- No header row, because a header would be treated as an entry
- Blank lines and lines without a comma are ignored
- Columns after the second are ignored
- Spaces around each value are trimmed

#+begin_example
猫,cat
犬,dog
水,water
#+end_example

* Settings reference

** Application tab

| Section       | Setting                                   | Default     |
|---------------+-------------------------------------------+-------------|
| Card          | Word font and size                        | System, 21 pt |
| Card          | Translation font and size                 | System, 16 pt |
| Card          | Fade when the pointer rests on the card   | On          |
| Rotation      | Show each card for                        | 8 seconds   |
| Recall        | Start recall automatically                | On          |
| Recall        | Recall every                              | 30 minutes  |
| Pronunciation | Speak cards in the rotation               | On          |
| Pronunciation | Speak cards during recall                 | On          |
| Pronunciation | Speak                                     | Word only   |
| Pronunciation | Voice                                     | Automatic   |
| General       | Start LexiCards at login                  | Off         |

The two pronunciation switches only take effect while *Pronounce Cards* is on in the menu.

* Limitations

- Commas can't appear inside a value, because quoted CSV fields aren't supported.
- Vocabularies can't be edited in the app. The entry table is read-only.
- Only one vocabulary is active at a time.
- Recall doesn't track progress or schedule entries by how well you know them.

* Troubleshooting

** macOS blocks the first launch

Releases are self-signed and not notarized. See [[file:../README.org][the README]] for how to allow the app.

** The card ignores clicks

It has probably faded under the pointer. Hold *Option*, or turn off fading in Settings.

** The app beeps

A beep means something failed, such as a file that couldn't be read. The reason is in the system log:

#+begin_src sh
log stream --predicate 'subsystem == "com.andreypudov.LexiCards"'
#+end_src
